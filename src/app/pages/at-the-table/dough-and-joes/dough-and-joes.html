Dough and Joes is the perfect mix of whimsy and classy!

A cafe that brings you the campy aesthetic? You cannot find that anywhere!

When you talk about a cafe, it is very important that in today’s world that you also acknowledge the aesthetics of a cafe restaurant because as much as the food plays a major role in getting customers, the aesthetic of the place also plays a major role in getting customers. And especially in a place like Bandra, which is unfortunately oversaturated with cafes at every corner, it can get very difficult to spot a good one amongst all. Other than Mokai, one more cafe, which I really appreciate and enjoy is Dough and Joes which is located at Hill Road in Bandra.

This cafe, just like any other cafe in Bandra, started getting popular on Instagram because of its very whimsy and vintage looking aesthetics. To be very honest, I have eaten at many eateries, but I have not seen any cafe that can match the decor that Dough and Joes has.

Just like anyone else watching multiple reels of this cafe on Instagram, I was intrigued to go there and I did, and now I’ve gone there twice, and I must say it is obviously a place that is 10 out of 10 in aesthetic sense, but food wise it surprised me. Because let’s be very honest, it might be a cliche, but cafes or restaurants that have a brilliant and out of the box aesthetic usually lack in food taste and menu. But Dough and Joes proved to be completely opposite. It is a cafe that is inspired by a lot of Italian food as well as a mixture of English food like fish and chips being one of the most popular items on the menu.

But before I dive into the food, I think it’s important to acknowledge the founders. The team behind this cafe is quite young and warm. Priyanka and Kushal Kotak are the other founders behind this very aesthetic cafe. They call this cafe, a child-like joy. For them, Dough and Joes is not just a cafe, but it is a neighbourhood Plaza. It has the aroma of fresh food and the sense of no rush. This is a space that is not just a cafe, but not even a restaurant. It’s somewhere in the middle and it is very difficult to find such spaces because when you are at a cafe there is a rush and when you are at a restaurant, there is also a rush, but at this place, there is a sense of calmness which is surprising and weird at first, but you get used to it once you start enjoying the delicious food that comes up on your table.

The cafe is located at the highest floor of the building, which also allows it to have its own terrace. They have a lot of cute white, red, black, blue and beige aesthetic sense which can be seen through their colour block tiles, whimsy and vintage furniture mirrors as well as the entryway which has a retro TV, which allows you to take pictures and I think that is the perfect catch to grab attention of the customers and especially viewers on social media to come and check this cafe out.

Outside, that is the terrace has this beautiful checker vibe, almost like a very elegant chess sense to it. Overall, the aesthetic of the cafe is the first thing that pulls you in, and you immediately are in a trance with such surprising elements and start to get the vibe, and honestly, it’s quite exciting

The menu is focused around pizza. Although very small, the menu offers a lot of Italian based food with fusion of Indian flavours in it. According to me the best dish that they have on their menu is the mozzarella sticks. It is the most perfect version of cheese sticks that I have ever seen because first of all the outside is so crunchy, and the inside is such that you can go 20 meters apart, but the cheese will still not break. That is the magic and the experience that you get at Dough and Joes.

Another great dish that they have is the fish and chips which I think are a great combination and obviously the main star of the show is the pizza. They have a good handful options for pizza and I think, although the menu is small for pizza, rather it’s quite small in general, but the food and the taste really delivers for it, and I think it’s very important to acknowledge it because some people might get disappointed by looking at the size of the menu which can be happen, but at the same time, I think once you order the food and eat it really blows apart.

And another thing about this cafe is obviously with the food, it also has a lot of merchandise which people might not like to buy any kind of merchandise from a restaurant or from an eatery, but I have the bag which Dough and Joes sells and I have been using it ever since I got it, and it’s one of the most fabulous bags. First of all, it is quite sustainable. It is whimsy as well as it’s quite fun looking and I think this is where the point of branding becomes so important today with any kind of company. Even if it’s just an eatery or if it’s a multimillion business, branding is very important, especially with your colours, your fonts, with your merchandise and much more.

Dough and Joes is a cafe that is sort of a hypnotic experiment, which pulls you in with aesthetics, but you stay for the food. It is a cafe that does not rush you. You can eat at your own pace and manage your own time. No one is gonna be behind you running. So I think this is a space that at least you should go to once rather than you should visit every cafe at least once, and if you like it, you might stick around and go for a second time just like I did, and honestly it’s a cafe that I will always go back to.
