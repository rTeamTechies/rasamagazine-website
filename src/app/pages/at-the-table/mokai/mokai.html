A taste of Oriental, right here in Bandra!

Bandra’s favourite eatery just got upgraded!

Photo credits - Zomato

Located in the heart of Pali Hill in Bandra is the very exciting and newly opened cafe Mokai. Started by Kareena Bulchandani, Mokai is a cafe, which is an experiential cafe, which blends in a lot of Japanese flavours, bringing it to the Indian and especially Mumbai palate. Kareena having lived and studied across China, Singapore and London, she wanted to bring in a sense of hospitality which she had seen in cities across Asia to the city of Mumbai. She has put in a lot of focus on more design forward, culture cafe scenes, where the concept of storytelling and individuality play a strong role in the hospitality industry. Mokai was started a long time back in April 2022, on Chapel Road in Bandra West. It gained a lot of momentum in the year 2025 when the cafe decided to do seasonal decorations for Christmas which brought in thousands of customers almost every day. Mokai has never been a static eatery, but is something that evolves along with seasons, time to stay relevant by introducing new menus, new flavour profiles and decor.

Of the most important reasons that Mokai received a lot of fame was its impeccable marketing technique that was started on Instagram. They have implemented a series just like the television series The Office where the characters and the employees play around with stories, creating episodes for every reel that they post. The main star of this series is Kareena’s right hand employee called Suraksha. She has taken the internet crowd for fire with her spitball roasting techniques and her carefree attitude. The Chapel Hill Road outlet was closed for renovation recently and a new outlet in Pali Hill was opened on March 19, 2026. The outlet also known as Mokai 2.0 is a larger and more advanced aesthetic cafe. The aesthetic of the cafe remains with the Japanese sense, but they have added in a Matcha bar where you can create your own matcha.

Mokai has always been famous for their matcha where they have a separate menu for Matcha and one can say that they were one of the first few torch bearers to bring in this matcha revolution, especially for the crowd of Mumbai. The cafe is such that it instantly pulls you in with aesthetic, music and the tasty menu. Right from Continental, Japanese and Korean inspired food to your delicious matcha, you can get everything under the sun. Mokai 2.0 quickly has become a space for a lot of influencers to come in and try out and post reels about this place. However, Mokai has always faced one issue that is waiting. From my personal experience, the first time we went to Mokai was in Chapel Road and we had to wait for almost 40 minutes, which was similar to the second time we went to the new outlet in Pali Hill, which was around 25 minutes of waiting. But the food, the decor and the ambience makes up for all the waiting that is done. Mokai is one of the first few cafes which is largely run by women and for Kareena it was very important to have a cafe which was largely led by women because according to her a lot of cafes in certain roles are quite male dominated, and she wanted to create a space where women felt equally confident while stepping into these positions as it can be seen in their social media marketing.

To anyone coming to Bandra or Mumbai for the first time, going to Mokai is a must especially for the food, for their ambience, and if you want, you can go and take a selfie with Suraksha herself! She is quite literally the star of Mokai and we have to acknowledge that such cafes are important because it only highlights and enhances as well as adds more to the palate and flavour of Mumbai itself.
