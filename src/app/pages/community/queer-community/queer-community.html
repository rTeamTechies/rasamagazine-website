The wonders of the queer community

With changing developments or progression happening in the world, it can be very confusing to call this as a positive progression because of certain grey areas that have come up, especially in humanity, humanitarian life and the way we identify or live.

In these troubling times, especially for this very important month of June, that is the Pride Month, it is very important that we give a moment and celebrate the queer community today. Irrespective of who you are, where you come from, or how you identify, whatever your background, your upbringing, your beliefs and ideologies are, you should have the basic Idea and the acceptance that every human, irrespective of all these elements has the right to exist and exist happily. This month allows us to remember all the hardships that people from the queer community have to face in order to just get accepted into society, get accepted by their families and friends to just be who they are. Today, the queer community faces a lot of problems, especially with a lot of societal fractions, creating trouble for the community, whether it is some law that has been passed or it is some other community, getting frustrated or angry for no reason with the queer community.

But one needs to think about how much the life of every human individual that exists on this planet, today in all aspects has changed because of the queer community. Meryl Streep has rightly quoted that without the queer community, there would be no fashion at all. Without the queer community, the interest that people have for music, largely pop music would not exist. Without the queer community, it would be very impossible to find people who are so advocating for other humans. Because the community people know how hard it is to live life, get accepted from others and not worry about anything. It is the queer community that is on the front largely fighting for the environment. It is the queer community that is bringing changes in the world of arts and culture. It is the queer community that is advocating for basic humanity, human laws and the right to live for other deprived people.

And in these times, we need to acknowledge the wonderful work that they have done to make sure that a legacy is continued irrespective of the hardship the community has had to face. Queer individuals are different from others in the sense that they are more resilient, they are stronger, they have more power and determination to make sure that irrespective of what they have to go through that they will succeed in life and get the chance to live as happily as others. It is very important that a common person goes beyond these aspects of sexuality, religion, class, economic conditions, and sees the person inside and that is something that the queer community does, they do not care about all these aspects because they know the person inside is hurting and so are they, but that common bond can be created into a magical bond of healing.

The queer community is diverse. They consist of different queer personas such as the gays, bisexuals, lesbians, transgender, asexual, pansexual, non-binary and much more. Although it might be difficult for a common person to find that adaptability to these concepts of pronouns and how to address people, but somewhere you can at least acknowledge and tolerate them. No one is telling you to open your arms and forget about everything and except everyone. This is a big change for the queer individual as well as the people around them to accept their identity, their pronouns and how they want to be addressed. But at the same time, it is very important, that the queer individual also realises that this is a change for people surrounding them and it will take some time for them to adjust it so you cannot get mad so quickly if someone migenders you or call you from the wrong pronoun. People have grown up with a certain idea of how a man looks like, or how a woman should look so it will take time for them to adjust to call you, a she or him. But know deep down in your heart that you need to remain positive because at the end of the day, it is the positivity that spreads out and influences others. If you are positive, then slowly the environment around you will become positive and the people will change, giving you that acceptance that you want.

Such thoughts and ideologies of the queer individuals of resilience, strength, power, ambition, dedication, and helping one another is something that is required in the world of leadership today. It is very important that we see and acknowledge that young leaders especially young queer leaders might be able to take a position and help people as leaders across different backgrounds and create a positive change for everyone. People like Gabriel Attal, Ana Brnabić, Anish Gawande, Prince Manvendra Singh Gohil are some of the key leaders out there who are creating change at a very high position in the field of politics and leadership today with openly sexuality identity disclosure. These people act as examples of how it is important to acknowledge your identity which also gives that sense of good difference. These wonderful people act as reminders for everyone, especially for the queer community that leaders from the queer community are required today because they are the ones who are at the forefront standing up for humanity, standing for the nature, standing for animals and standing up for each other, which is very important.

This allows for a more comprehensive lookout in the field of politics and leadership today, as it gives a platform to people coming from different walks of life to share their identity, the problems that people from their community are facing and much more, which only allows for development and progression, which unfortunately is something that is missing today.

One needs to understand the joy that is there to be part of the queer community, and that only happens when you are an ally or you are yourself a part of the queer community. It is very important that common people accept, acknowledge, tolerate and love individuals from all walks of life. Pride Month serves as a reminder for everyone to understand and realise the wonders that one queer person can bring about so many positive changes in your life or a larger stage. Celebrate your pride, celebrate your queer identity and make sure it keeps shining through!
