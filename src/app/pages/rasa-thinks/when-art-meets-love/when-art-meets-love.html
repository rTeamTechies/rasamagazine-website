When Art meets Love

Why live performances nourish the soul and connect us to culture.

Photo credits - Tallenge Stores

In today’s quick world, a world where we are lost in the touch and importance of cultural events like musical concerts, theatre performances, or dance performances become more than just entertainment. They become a way of reconnecting with our culture, community and oneself as well. Different art forms provide a space for inspiration, reflection, as well as a sense of emotional renewal.

A live performance is a totally different experience. It engages all the senses, offering an experience that no recording or screen can replicate the atmosphere of a concert hall, a theatre, either grand or intimate. It’s always filled with anticipation, collective silence, and shared applause, which then creates a beautiful relation between the artist and audience that is deeply human as well as appreciative of the culture.

Artistic events do their job of preserving tradition while also encouraging innovation. A musical concert of Hindustani music or Carnatic music or Bharatanatyam recital introduces us to centuries-old traditions, heritage while contemporary theatre pushes us to think critically about modern issues as well. Such art forms provide a place for dialogue between the past, the present, the tradition as well as experimentation.

For some individuals, such artistic events not only help them find oneself but also nurture creativity, empathy and emotional intelligence. For communities, they help foster a sense of cultural identity, inclusivity and pride. By supporting the arts, the artist, as well as the tradition is very important as they are the pillars of our culture which are holding our long built culture strongly, which is the very fabric of society.

Ultimately, attending arts events helps us remember that beauty, meaning and connection are essential to humans in life as food and shelter. A monotonous grey life without any colour of culture is bland, and thus we should do our duty in maintaining and preserving our culture by supporting such artists and arts.
