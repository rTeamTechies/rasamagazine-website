The very anticipated film of this New Year 2026, The Devil Wears Prada 2, is releasing soon in theatres on May 1st. Something very exciting about this film is that obviously the film is going to be a cultural reset for this generation, but at the same time, the music of this film is gonna be something that is going to completely change music in films. When the first film was released in 2006, the iconic song by Madonna, Vogue, was included in this film and it instantly became a hit. Similarly, there were quite a few rumours about who is gonna be featured on the new track. The suspicions and rumours started to become true when Lady Gaga was seen at The Devil Wears Prada 2 premiere in New York, which confirmed that the singer had a part and track in the film along with the rapper Doechii.

Just before the film is going to air in the new month, the new and very much anticipated track called Runway by Lady Gaga and Doechii was released on April 27, 2026. The entire track is similar to the vibes of Vogue by Madonna, but almost in an upgraded and reupholstered way. The music video is directed by Parris Goebel & can be considered as a high fashion club ready video, which is completely full of couture packed scenes.

For this Runway music video, the Grammy award winning artists wore custom made Gaurav Gupta couture. These were two handcrafted body suits, which featured almost 3000 crystals, creating a striking and immersive look and creating a statement, nonetheless.

To talk about Gaurav Gupta is never enough. He is one of India’s most celebrated contemporary fashion designers who is popular for completely innovating & defining fashion couture through a very futuristic and artistic perspective. He is based in New Delhi and co-founded his label in 2005 with his brother Saurabh Gupta. He completed his education in the National Institute of Fashion Technology and Central Saint Martins in London. The fashion House of Gaurav Gupta started its journey as a very experimental label, which challenged traditional Indian silhouettes. Over the course of time, it has definitely evolved into a deserved globally recognised brand with multiple stores across the nation and internationally as well. It involves luxury retailers like Bergdorf Goodman and Neiman Marcus. Gaurav Gupta’s work has been showcased at multiple global platforms like Paris fashion week, making it a significant milestone for Indian designers on the global stage.

Gaurav’s fashion is widely known for his sculptural, avant garde aesthetic, which merges Indian craftsmanship, such as Zardozi embroidery with different futuristic architectural forms. His creations are such that they come from the background of surrealism, mythology and spirituality, which results in different outfits, challenging conventional clothing. His fame has been amplified by the strong presence on multiple global red carpets by celebrities such as Beyoncé, Cardi B, Lizzo, and Deepika Padukone and many more. Gaurav Gupta’s work is such that it stands out for a lot of experimentation, technological innovation, and one of the most important aspects of artistic storytelling.

For this particular collaboration between Doechii and Lady Gaga, each garment was created with almost 800 hours of meticulous handwork executed by a team of more than 20 skilled artisans. The detailing in the outfits is extraordinary with thousands and thousands of crystals shining under the light resulting in a dimensional finish.

Lady Gaga adorned a pearl toned fully embellished corset bodysuit, which is encrusted with crystals and pearls while Doechii wore a black corseted body suit, layered with spikes and metal studs over a base of sequins and crystals. Both the looks are made with single piece silhouettes with integrated face coverings, extending from head to toe to merge the body, garment and mask. One can definitely see the hard work that has gone into creating these outfits because the way the music video also captures the beauty of the outfits is extraordinary. In a way the way the outfits are also styled, with the two ponytails coming out of Doechii’s mask, talks a lot about individual personality and styling.
