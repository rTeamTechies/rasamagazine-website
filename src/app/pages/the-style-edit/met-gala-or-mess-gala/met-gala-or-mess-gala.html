The Style Edit

Met Gala or Mess Gala? Cannes or Can-not? What’s going on?

Was the Met Gala a flop? Or was it Cannes? Here’s my take on it.

Photo credits - Vogue

Fashion is something that is always important when you talk about a red carpet. For me fashion needs to be natural, it should not be forced. It should be something that should make you look good, feel good and make the person looking at you feel good. I might not have any credentials in fashion, but I am someone who has opinions and hence I decided to write this article for the fashion section for Rasa.

The Met Gala is the biggest fashion event that happens every year on the first Monday of May. Every year the Met Gala announces different themes and celebrities show up wearing different outfits relating to that theme or rather somewhere near that and it is an event which is basically a charity event to collect money for the sustenance of the Metropolitan museum in New York. The theme for this year was Fashion is Art and it’s safe to say just like every other year, hardly any celebrity showed up in an outfit on theme.

At first it seems a very controversial senseless event, but for fashion enthusiasts, fashion designers, and fashion students. This is their most important day. Different designers team up with different celebrities and show their work on the carpet. For this issue, it is very difficult for me to even find a good top 10, but somehow I have managed to shift my criteria for a good outfit and here are the top 5 outfits of the Met Gala 2026, according to me.

Cannes is a film festival that occurs every year where movies from all over the world are put up for jury and according to different categories that are winners. It is a very prestigious festival, but somewhere down the line, the focus of this festival has shifted to fashion where a lot of celebrities at least from India go with the mentality that this is a fashion festival and not a film festival.

Something I like to point out which is so off topic is the occurrence of these influencers who are not even known by the photographers there on the red carpet of Cannes festival. They pay a huge amount of money to get 7 to 8 photographs, one video to wear some designer looks that are below appreciation level and achieve something that doesn’t exist. Nonetheless, the main important people that actually attend these events, such as the actors. who actually are serious about the work, directors, producers, and many more from all over the world show up in decent outfits. It is very important to understand and acknowledge that this is not a festival which regards and associates itself with fashion. The fashion part of it is something that is brought by the celebrity attending the festival. It is not a fashion event, but a film event and people who are passionate about films are over there. Here are top 5 looks that I found quite interesting and good enough to be on this list.

The Met Gala

Madonna in Yves Saint Laurent

This was an unexpected look for me. When you talk about taking inspiration from the art, this is also something that comes to one’s mind. Madonna took inspiration from the surrealist painting, the Temptation of St. Anthony by artist Leonora Carrington. The dress she wore is a Gothic inspired outfit with a sheer long train, black hair extensions, opera gloves, and a beautiful structural top hat, which resembles a sailboat. She had her group of ladies in waiting, holding the sheer and dramatic cape which really brought in that effect of drama that was sort of missing from the red carpet. Although the outfit is not extremely great, it’s the inspiration and the entire ensemble along with the ladies waiting that pulls the look together and make it a good outfit to include in this list.

Audrey Nuna in Robert Wun

Making her first appearance at the Met Gala, Audrey wore an art inspired custom piece by the designer Robert Wun from his FW25 couture collection. The outfit is heavily inspired by abstract art and has mysterious stains which only appear under UV lighting. The entire ensemble features a pristine white court dress with a matching hat also including splatters of 15,000 jet black Swarovski crystals, which depict and mimic a Jackson Pollock style drip painting. The entire ensemble was quite beautiful to look at, especially nothing can go wrong with the colour combination of white and black, and when you put it in an extremely chic outfit like this, I am in love. Especially wearing an outfit that is so couture and camp, and in a way that it is almost work wear with the particular collars and the structural shape, it really gives that quintessential fashion energy.

Heidi Klum

When you talk about literal art, this is what comes to mind with Heidi Klum showing up as a marble statue. She is the Queen of Halloween and although not a surprise, this is something you’d not expect on the Met Gala carpet, but she did that, and she gagged everyone. She collaborated with the prosthetic artist, Mike Marino to create a convincing costume, which is made of foam, latex and spandex. She was inspired by the classical 19th century veiled sculptures, and especially the work of Italian sculptor, Raffaele Monti. There is something so camp about this look that you cannot even describe it. It is so out there so unexpected and so unusual and it gives a very refreshing perspective on fashion, especially on the most fashionable red carpet, that is the Met Gala. I love her commitment to pulling this off and looking like a culture.

Janelle Monae in Christian Siriano

The 2026 Met Gala theme was Fashion is Art. When you think of this theme, the first thing that comes to mind is camp, fashionable, exquisite with an element of whimsy and Janelle Monet's look serves exactly that. It’s a high fashion gown, if you remove the attachments of the wires and the moss. And the way the moss and the wires are placed, they create such intricate patterns making the garment another shape. The black wires and white wires compliment the green moss, giving that apocalyptic look which adds that flavour of camp to this high fashion garment.

Anok Yai in Balenciaga

Although this outfit was not my favourite, it is the face, and this couture elements that really pushes me over the edge. Her outfit was inspired by the black Madonna and the Mater Dolorosa. She had incorporated detailed prosthetics into her make-up and hand carved hair done by Sasha Glasser, which gave that extra effect to the entire outfit. She also had these gold tears, which was so striking against the black gown that also was quite cultural in a way that really brought an essence of high-level fashion to the red carpet. Her outfit was a custom gown designed in collaboration with Pierpaolo Piccioli.

Cannes Film Festival

Sharon Stone in Miss Sohee

And I think this is the most beautiful gown, I have seen grace the red carpet and I did not even go over all the 300 looks that were there. Everything about this gown is exquisite. Everything. Everything about this dress is exquisite. The hombre from the light blue to the dark blue panel. The White at the front and the column design. The bedazzle works with the crystals right on the cape and the bodice. The embroidery with the periwinkle flowers on the front. Everything about this outfit is perfect to the T. The accessories with the necklace, the matching clutch and a simple make up look, really adds more flavour to this ensemble. The bedazzled crystal bead work is done so perfectly that it is not overshadowing the outfit, rather it is complimenting it so beautifully with the way, especially with the it is placed, the amount of bead work that is done as well. The simple yet beautiful periwinkle flowers all along the outfit really add that softness. The inside of the cap is lined with white giving that contrast yet complimentary look that is so intricate and exquisite. This sleeveless gown and cape combination is always something that works so beautifully when it’s done right and this is an example of how to exactly do it. Hands down my favourite look from the carpet.

Aishwarya Rai Bachchan wearing Amit Agarwal

For this first look for her first 2026 appearance, Aishwarya adorned a blue Amit Agarwal ensemble, which has the signature style of Crystal Veiled embroidery technique which is developed over more than 1500 hours of hand work. The outfit has thousands of crystalline embellishments connected by lattice structures. Aishwarya‘s appearance was something that was quite anticipated, and this look was something that was so different. Aishwarya’s appearance this year was something more magical, and it could be noticed that she felt more confident, natural, and in her own skin with these outfits, and it was so beautiful to see because the garment is beautiful. It is a simple silhouette, but the embellishments, that colour, the styling the necklace, all of it goes together in a very beautiful ensemble look. There are structural elements yet the outfit is flowing, giving it more space and meaning to the look.

Cate Blanchett in Givenchy

This is someone who amazes me every time she’s on the red carpet. A lot of people don’t acknowledge her ability to serve a look, and I honestly don’t know why. For me, she is the total queen of Cannes . This look is so exquisite, so fun, so couture. It’s a painted, embroidered and shredded backless gown made by the creative director of Givenchy, Sarah Burton. The backless gown is met with bare shoulders and the voluminous fabric at the forearms and especially the colour arrangement with a black base and beautiful orange shaded flowers with the fringe gives it an exquisite feeling. This is a look that is taken from the design collection of fall 2026 Read to Wear showcase. The styling of the look with the minimal makeup and a sleek bun is everything because the attention is all on the outfit and it does everything.

Eva Longoria wearing Tamara Ralph couture

Out of the several looks that Eva wore, this is my favourite because there is something so ethereal yet so whimsical about this look. The bodice looks like a shell with the beautiful silver lining and the rest of the gown is simple in the way that the shell looking bodice pulls more of the focus. The simplicity of the gown is what makes it beautiful, the clashing white with the tan brown skin, beautiful necklace, and the shoes which were satin sandals, make the entire look beautiful and simple.

Demi Moore in Matière Fécales

Out of all the looks that Demi Moore wore for the Cannes festival, which were a little more structural and snatched and simple, this one was the one that caught my eye. There is something so different with an aesthetic oversized bow and layers and layers of tulle skirt. The look is very similar and gives the vibe of her hit film The Substance, but it is so beautifully paired with stunning earrings. The torn style and the hot pink colour are elements really pulling together and it’s something so wonderful to see something fun and camp on the red carpet.
